# Code of Conduct

We want BicLex Hub to be a welcoming and practical open-source project.

Be respectful, assume good intent, discuss technical decisions with evidence, and give actionable feedback. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not accepted in issues, pull requests, discussions, or other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately through the contact listed at [biclex.ru](https://biclex.ru/).
